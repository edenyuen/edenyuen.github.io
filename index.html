Acai Bowls are the Best

By Eden Yuen

What are Acai Bowls?

An acai bowl consists of a blend of frozen acai puree, blended with liquid like almond milk or coconut water, and other frozen fruits such as bananas or berries to create a thick base.

What are the Benefits?

Acai berries are rich in antioxidants and nutrients.

Most acai bowls contain far more than just acai. Add-ins can quickly change the nutrition profile.

You can add onto your acai bowl with any toppings of your choosing!
